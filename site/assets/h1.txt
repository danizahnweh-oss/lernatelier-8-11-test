Hello everyone. Here is some information about our London photography project on Saturday. Please meet outside the library at ten thirty. Our first message said ten o’clock, but the exhibition opens later, so ten thirty is the new time. Bring a pencil and a notebook. You do not need a camera because we will look at photographs that local teenagers have already taken. In pairs, choose one photograph and discuss what it tells you about the neighbourhood. At twelve, we will meet again inside the library, next to the information desk. Please do not wait at the entrance. If you arrive late, ask a member of staff to show you the exhibition room. Finally, remember that the people in the photographs have agreed to take part in this fictional school project. Do not take or share new photographs of visitors without asking them.
